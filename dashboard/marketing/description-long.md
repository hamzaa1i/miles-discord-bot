# aurelia ✦ the soft, elegant discord bot

**ai chat that remembers you.** talk to her three ways — slash commands,
natural language (@aurelia remind me in 10m to stretch), or a classic
prefix. she keeps the thread of your conversations, learns the durable
facts worth keeping, and can even chime in on her own in channels you
choose. every ai feature is opt-out per user, per server.

**aesthetic welcome cards.** embed, text, hybrid or dm modes with custom
colors, images and template variables — with a live preview before you
save. goodbye messages included.

**gentle but firm moderation.** warnings as numbered cases with
escalating thresholds (timeout → kick → ban, all configurable),
context-aware ai automod that scores severity 1-5 and only escalates
when it should, full server event logging and a searchable case history.

**the whole engagement toolkit.** leveling with role rewards and a
leaderboard, daily streak rewards, the question of the day with answer
threads, giveaways with realtime entry counts, a starboard, anonymous
confessions, ship compatibility, achievements, polls, custom commands
and more — 169 commands across 46 modules.

**a dashboard as pretty as your server.** 34 module pages wrapped in
veloura: live discord channel/role pickers, one-click live actions
("post qotd now", "test welcome"), realtime updates and a full audit
log. configure everything visually at veloura-aurelia.vercel.app — slash
commands keep working alongside.

**privacy first.** minimal data by design, no analytics, no third-party
trackers. /privacy export gives you everything as json; /privacy delete
erases it everywhere after double confirmation.

**free forever.** no paywalls, no premium tiers, no ads, no locked
features. MIT-licensed and self-hostable if you prefer your own
infrastructure.

add aurelia — she'll remember why you did ♡
