aurelia is the soft, elegant discord bot for communities that care about
vibes. she chats with members through ai that actually remembers you,
greets every newcomer with aesthetic welcome cards, and moderates gently
but firmly — warnings with escalating thresholds plus context-aware ai
automod. underneath: leveling with role rewards, daily streaks, the
question of the day, giveaways with live entry counts, a starboard,
anonymous confessions, ships, achievements and a full 34-page web
dashboard wrapped in veloura. privacy-first by design (export or delete
your data anytime), self-hostable under MIT, and 100% free forever — no
premium tiers, no ads, no locked commands.
